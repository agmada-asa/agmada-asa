# 👋 Hello, I’m Allen

I'm a second year Computer Science student at the University of Manchester. I finished first year with an **84% average**, ranked 7th of 300+, and was awarded the **Netcraft Prize**.

I build AI agents, small language models and the apps around them. I founded [Mixhaus](https://mixhaus.app/), took it through the Manchester Venture Builder programme, and I design and run workshops and hackathons with the Manchester AI Society.

## 🛠️ Technical Skills

- **Languages:** Python, TypeScript, Java, Kotlin, Swift
- **AI and ML:** PyTorch, Transformers, Hugging Face, LlamaIndex (RAG), Whisper, Ollama
- **Web and cloud:** Next.js, React, Node.js, Supabase, Firebase, Cloudflare R2
- **Agents:** Claude Agent SDK, Codex app-server, MCP

## 📌 Featured projects

- **[NanoMath-R](https://github.com/agmada-asa/NanoMath-R)**: a 103.8M-parameter recurrent Transformer for mathematical reasoning, built from scratch in PyTorch. It runs 16 weight-shared blocks over two passes. Weights and reproducible benchmarks are public.
- **[Mixhaus](https://mixhaus.app)**: a cross-platform app where music producers and engineers keep every version of a mix and collect client feedback. I built and ran it alone, from engineering to sales.
- **[Blitz](https://github.com/agmada-asa/Blitz)**: a study app that teaches your course material back to you in live sessions, drills it and runs mock exams. Open source under MIT.
- **[agent-engines](https://github.com/agmada-asa/agent-engines)**: a TypeScript library that runs Codex and Claude agents behind one API, on the subscriptions already signed in on the machine. Published on npm, and it runs the agents in Blitz.
- **[NanoMath](https://github.com/agmada-asa/NanoMath)**: a 136M-parameter GPT-style model trained on algorithmic arithmetic, with structured reasoning traces.
- **[PhoneUse](https://github.com/agmada-asa/PhoneUse)**: lets a local coding agent use an Android phone over your own Wi-Fi through MCP, with on-device consent, an app blocklist and no cloud relay.
- **[Tide](https://github.com/agmada-asa/Tide)**: an Android music player in Kotlin that gets your library right even when the tags are a mess, and imports Spotify playlists by matching them to files on your phone.
